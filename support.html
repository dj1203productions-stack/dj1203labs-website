Help and support

Stuck on something? Email labs@dj1203productions.com and tell us what you were doing and what happened. Screenshots help. Inside Intuimix, the ? button at the top opens the full guide.

Installing

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

The beta isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows doesn’t recognize it. Click More info, then Run anyway. This goes away once signing is in place.

My Mac says the app can’t be opened

The beta isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Try to open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Intuimix. On older macOS versions you can instead right-click Intuimix in Applications and choose Open. You only need to do this once.

Which Mac download do I need?

Apple menu, About This Mac. If it says Apple M1 or newer, use the arm64 download. If it says Intel, use the x64 one.

Using Intuimix

Where do my sets show up in my DJ app?

Press Send to under your set and pick your DJ app. Your sets appear in an Intuimix folder (a crate in Serato), named the way you named them. Intuimix shows where to look in each app after it sends.

Will it change my existing cue points or tags?

No, not unless you ask. Syncing only fills empty hot cue pads and adds what’s missing. Replacing anything is a separate switch with a warning first, and a backup is saved before any DJ app file changes.

Does it upload my music?

No. Your music stays on your computer. See Privacy for what is sent when you use AI picking or lyrics.

Which DJ apps does it work with?

Serato DJ Pro, rekordbox, Traktor Pro, VirtualDJ, Engine DJ and djay Pro.
