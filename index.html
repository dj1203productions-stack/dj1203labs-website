Intuimix

Built by a working DJ, for the gig in front of you.

DJ1203 Labs grew out of DJ1203 Productions, a working mobile DJ business. Intuimix is the tool we wanted for weddings, parties and club nights: fast to learn, and serious once you're in.

Draw the energy, get the set

Sketch how the night should rise and fall, or say it in words. Intuimix picks tracks from your library to match, with waveforms for every song, and you can swap any one in real time.

One button to your DJ app

Send a finished set to rekordbox, Serato DJ Pro, VirtualDJ, Traktor Pro, Engine DJ or djay Pro, with its cue points. It shows up in an Intuimix folder, named the way you named it. Your existing cue points and tags are never overwritten unless you say so.

Mix in harmony with the Key Wheel

Hover a song and its compatible keys light up. Prefer traditional key names? Switch with one setting.

Simple, Advanced or Expert

Start with the basics and step up when you're ready. Every level keeps the same easy steps.

Clean up your library

Tag Fixer repairs messy and missing tags, and only writes to your files when you say so. Double Take finds songs you have twice and files that have gone missing.

Your own AI, if you want it

Describe the night in plain words and your AI (Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT and others) picks from your library. Setup is three steps, and Gemini has a free tier. Without AI, Intuimix still builds great sets.

Your first set in three steps

1

Add your music

Point Intuimix at your music folders. It reads and analyzes what's there.

2

Build a set for a real gig

Pick the event, set the length, and shape the energy. Name it whatever you like.

3

Send it to your DJ app

Press Send, and the button turns green when it lands. Intuimix tells you where to find it.
