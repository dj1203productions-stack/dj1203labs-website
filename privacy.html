Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026. This page covers the Intuimix app and this website.

The Intuimix app

Your music files stay on your computer; the app does not upload them. The app sends information only when you use these features: AI picking and Ask AI send song details (such as artist, title, genre, BPM, key and year), what you ask for, and problem reports (which can include file and folder names) to the AI service you chose, using your own account with that service and under its terms; looking up lyrics sends the artist and title to a lyrics service; downloading a chart requests it from the chart’s publisher. You are responsible for any charges from those services.

Your music, your library, your sets and any AI service key you enter stay on your computer. Your streaming sign-ins go to TIDAL or SoundCloud, and requests to an AI service go to the AI company whose key you entered. Feedback, problem reports and crash details come to us only if you send them. We use what you send us only to improve Intuimix, and we don’t sell it.

This website

This site is a set of plain pages. It has no sign-up forms, no advertising, no analytics and no cookies of our own. Its pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit; see Google’s privacy policy for details. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as your IP address for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to run the beta, and we don’t sell it or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep emails, beta sign-ups and anything else you send us only as long as we need them to support you and run the beta, and then we delete them.

You can ask us what we have from you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing labs@dj1203productions.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

Intuimix and this website are for adults only. You must be at least 18 to use Intuimix or join the beta, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will also tell beta testers by email or in the app.
